Grammar Tips:
The present perfect tense is used to describe past actions that have relevance to the present.
For example: "I have lived in Beijing for five years" means I started living there five years ago and still live there now.

The past simple tense is used for completed actions in the past.
For example: "I visited Shanghai last year" means the visit is finished.
